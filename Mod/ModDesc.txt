[h1]Folktails+ Addons[/h1]
[b]Four independent Folktails+ addons for Timberborn 1.1[/b]

[b]Charging Station Boost[/b] — Folktails CollectionId: remove Energy, append ChargingBoost in the Boosts group. No global changes to Iron Teeth needs.
[b]Tiny Tubeway Station[/b] — Optional small tubeway station integration.
[b]Scientific Projects[/b] — Five optional science projects in Folktails.
[b]FT Mixer — Real Costs[/b] — Four mixed-faction building cost changes.

Use Mods → Folktails+ Addons → Settings to control features. Restart after changes.

[h1]v1.2.6[/h1]
ChargingBoost now uses a single conditional Folktails NeedCollectionSpec patch with Needs#remove Energy and Needs#append ChargingBoost. Previous faction-level / IronTeeth need modifier removed. The game's multiple-collection union may still surface Energy; in-game verification is required.

Install by deleting the previous FolktailsPlusAddons folder and extracting this one. Back up your save.
