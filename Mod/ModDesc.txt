[h1]Folktails+ Addons[/h1]
[b]One mod, four independent features, Timberborn 1.1.[/b]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[b]Charging Station Boost[/b] — Adds ChargingBoost to Folktails' need collection and removes inherited basic bot Energy for Folktails only. The bonus displays as Power under Boosts; Iron Teeth colonies remain unchanged.
[b]Tiny Tubeway Station[/b] — Integrates Tiny Tubeway Station when its companion mod is installed.
[b]Scientific Projects[/b] — Makes five Iron Teeth scientific projects available to Folktails with the companion science mods.
[b]FT Mixer — Real Costs[/b] — Applies mixed-faction cost balancing to four Iron Teeth buildings.

[h1]Configuration[/h1]
Each feature has an independent, enabled-by-default setting through Mod Settings. Missing optional mods disable only their corresponding feature. Restart Timberborn after changing settings.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
Folktails+ 11+, Mod Settings 1.1.1+, TimberUi 11+. Tiny Tubeway Station, Scientific Projects and Alternative Recipes SPs optional.

[h1]v1.2.5[/h1]
Restores correct Power/ChargingBoost behavior with native, faction-scoped JSON modifiers. Explicit CollectionId Folktails receives ChargingBoost; Energy is removed only from IronTeeth needs inherited by Folktails. No full collection replacement or global modification of IronTeeth. Back up existing saves before testing.

[h1]Installation[/h1]
Delete the previous FolktailsPlusAddons folder completely before extracting this new single-mod ZIP.
