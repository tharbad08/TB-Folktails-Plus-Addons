[h1]Folktails+ Addons[/h1]
[b]One mod, four optional features, native game APIs, Timberborn 1.1.[/b]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[b]Charging Station Boost[/b] — For Folktails+ ONLY, remove Energy from the Iron Teeth bot need collection and append ChargingBoost under Boosts. Uses built-in faction-scoped JSON modifiers; leaves all other needs and Iron Teeth-only colonies untouched.
[b]Tiny Tubeway Station[/b] — Integrate Tiny Tubeway Station when the companion mod is enabled.
[b]Scientific Projects[/b] — Five Iron Teeth scientific projects in Folktails; requires Scientific Projects and Alternative Recipes Scientific Projects.
[b]FT Mixer — Real Costs[/b] — Mixed-faction prices for the Deep Water Pump, Deep Badwater Pump, Deep Mechanical Fluid Pump and Industrial Lumber Mill.

[h1]Configuration[/h1]
Separate enabled-by-default toggles under Mods → Folktails+ Addons → Settings. Optional dependencies are checked automatically. [b]Restart the game after changes.[/b]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
Folktails+ 11+, Mod Settings 1.1.1+, TimberUi 11+. Other companion mods are optional.

[h1]v1.2.4 – ChargingBoost regression fix[/h1]
No need-collection replacement. Uses the game's faction-specific BlueprintModifiers and a minimal JSON diff: Needs#remove Energy; Needs#append ChargingBoost. The normal IronTeeth needs are unchanged when playing Iron Teeth. Make a backup of existing saves before updating.

[h1]Install[/h1]
Completely remove the previous FolktailsPlusAddons folder before extracting the new single-folder package.
