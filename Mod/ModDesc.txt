[h1]Folktails+ Addons[/h1]
[b]One mod. Four configurable Folktails+ enhancements. Timberborn 1.1.[/b]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[b]Charging Station Boost[/b] — Restores the original Folktails+ ChargingBoost experience: Power appears under Boosts, not Basic Needs. Preserves the original 32-need Folktails+ collection, without replacing the Iron Teeth faction's needs.
[b]Tiny Tubeway Station[/b] — Adds Bobingabout's Tiny Tubeway Station to Folktails+ when its companion mod and blueprint exist.
[b]Scientific Projects[/b] — Makes five Iron Teeth scientific projects available for Folktails when both science companion mods are enabled.
[b]FT Mixer — Real Costs[/b] — Rebalances costs of Deep Water Pump, Deep Badwater Pump, Deep Mechanical Fluid Pump, and Industrial Lumber Mill.

[h1]Settings[/h1]
Each feature has a separate checkbox in Mods → Folktails+ Addons → Settings. Optional companions are detected through Timberborn's own mod APIs; missing mods disable only their features. [b]Restart after changing settings.[/b]

[h1]Dependencies[/h1]
[b]Required:[/b] Folktails+ 11+, Mod Settings 1.1.1+, TimberUi 11+.
[b]Optional:[/b] Tiny Tubeway Station, Scientific Projects, Alternative Recipes Scientific Projects.

[h1]Install[/h1]
Delete the previous FolktailsPlusAddons folder before installing this single-folder ZIP. Back up existing saves: the ChargingBoost feature replaces the Iron Teeth basic Energy need in Folktails with the original mod's custom wellbeing need. The mod does not modify the Iron Teeth faction's own need list and requires no file-writing permissions.

[h1]v1.2.3: Power category regression fixed[/h1]
v1.2.1/1.2.2 incorrectly kept the IronTeeth need collection attached to Folktails. v1.2.3 restores the complete original FolktailsPlus need collection, which contains ChargingBoost under Boosts and does not include the basic Energy need.
