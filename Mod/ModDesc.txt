[h1]Folktails+ Addons[/h1]
Four configurable Folktails+ extensions: Charging Station Boost, Tiny Tubeway Station, Scientific Projects and FT Mixer Real Costs.

[h1]v1.2.7 — Power category fix[/h1]
ChargingBoost is added to Folktails' Boosts and basic Energy is removed from the IronTeeth collection inherited through Folktails+. Only individual need IDs are modified; no collection is replaced. Native game blueprint modifier API, no file-writing hacks.

[b]When playing Iron Teeth, disable both Folktails+ and Folktails+ Addons and restart.[/b]
Delete the old addon folder before installing. Back up affected saves.

Required: Folktails+ 11+, Mod Settings 1.1.1+, TimberUi 11+.
